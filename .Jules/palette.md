## 2025-02-28 - Dual ARIA and Title Attributes on Modal and Drawer Close Buttons
**Learning:** Icon-only modal and drawer close buttons ('✕') communicate closure visually to sighted users via iconography, but without `aria-label` screen readers announce them as ambiguous unlabelled buttons or merely "multiplication sign". Furthermore, omitting the `title` attribute deprives mouse users of hover tooltips explaining the button's action. Adding both `aria-label="Close modal"` / `aria-label="Close navigation drawer"` and matching `title` attributes provides complete accessibility across assistive tech and pointer devices without cluttering the UI.
**Action:** Always pair `aria-label` and `title` attributes with clear action descriptions on icon-only close buttons across modals, dialogs, drawers, and popups.
