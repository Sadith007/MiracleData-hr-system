## 2026-09-03 - Icon-Only Button ARIA Accessibility
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons in static HTML markup (like `✕`, `↺`, `⧉`, `🌙`) often omit `aria-label` and `title` attributes, rendering them unannounced or ambiguous for screen reader users and missing native tooltip context on hover.
**Action:** Always ensure all icon-only buttons include explicit, descriptive `aria-label` and `title` attributes.
