## 2026-07-04 - Accessible Modal Dialog Structure and Close Controls
**Learning:** Modal dialogs in monolithic single-page apps need explicit ARIA dialog roles (`role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, and `aria-labelledby`) linked to their heading IDs, along with accessible labels and tooltips (`aria-label="Close modal"` and `title="Close modal"`) on header close buttons ('✕') so screen reader users receive clear contextual announcements when a dialog opens and can easily identify the dismissal control.
**Action:** Whenever adding or auditing modal containers, ensure each container has `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, and an `aria-labelledby` referencing the title element, and guarantee all icon-only close buttons provide explicit accessible labels and title tooltips.
